## 1. Overview

The **Non-Commercial Freeware License (NCL-FW)** is a software license designed for developers, researchers, and creators who wish to make their source code and applications publicly accessible and modifiable for non-commercial purposes, while strictly prohibiting unauthorized commercial exploitation, distribution, or monetization.

Under NCL-FW, individuals and non-profit organizations may freely inspect, modify, fork, and share the software. However, any entity seeking to derive direct or indirect financial gain, integrate the software into commercial infrastructure, or distribute it as part of a paid product must obtain prior explicit written authorization from the copyright holder.

The complete legal text of the license is available in [NCL-FW-LICENSE.md](NCL-FW-LICENSE.md).

## 4. Applying NCL-FW to Your Project

To apply this license to your software:

1. Copy the contents of [`NCL-FW-LICENSE.md`](NCL-FW-LICENSE.md) into a file named `LICENSE` or `LICENSE.md` in the root directory of your project.
2. Update the copyright header in the license file:
   ```text
   Copyright (c) [YEAR] [COPYRIGHT HOLDER NAME]. All rights reserved.
   ```
3. Update the contact address in **Section 4** to specify where commercial licensing inquiries should be directed.
4. (Optional) Include a brief notice at the top of individual source files:
   ```text
   Copyright (c) [YEAR] [COPYRIGHT HOLDER NAME].
   Licensed under the NCL-FW (Non-Commercial Freeware License) v1.0.
   Commercial use is strictly prohibited without explicit written authorization.
   See LICENSE for details.
   ```
